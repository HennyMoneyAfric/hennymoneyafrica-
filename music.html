🎤 THE SOUND

Music With an African Message

HennyMoney Afric creates Afrobeat music inspired by African culture, everyday experiences, unity, creativity and the desire to showcase Africa to international audiences.

Music is part of a larger creative mission that connects African music with culture, tourism, storytelling, digital creativity and entrepreneurship.

The goal is simple: connect Africa to the world through music.
