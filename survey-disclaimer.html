📌 Purpose of This Disclaimer

This page explains how survey and reward opportunities presented through HennyMoney Afric may operate and provides important information for participants.

The purpose is to maintain transparency and help visitors understand that survey opportunities may be provided by independent third-party research companies.

Participation is voluntary. You should participate only when you understand the applicable terms and are comfortable providing the information requested by the relevant research provider.

🤝 Third-Party Research Providers

HennyMoney Afric may work with or provide access to independent third-party research and survey providers.

These providers may independently operate surveys, determine participant eligibility, collect research responses, calculate rewards and process redemptions.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, HennyMoney Afric is not the operator of a third-party survey simply because that survey is accessible through or referenced by our website.

Always review the identity, terms, privacy policy and instructions of the applicable third-party provider before participating.

👤 Eligibility

Access to a survey does not guarantee eligibility to complete it.

Eligibility may depend on factors determined by the research provider, including location, age, demographic characteristics, research requirements, available survey quotas and other criteria.

A participant may be screened out of a survey even after beginning the survey. This can happen when the participant's responses do not match the requirements of the research study.

🎁 Rewards & Compensation

Some survey programmes may provide points, cash-equivalent rewards, gift cards, digital rewards or other forms of compensation for eligible participation.

The availability and value of rewards are determined by the applicable programme and may vary according to location, survey type, eligibility and other programme conditions.

Minimum redemption thresholds, processing periods, supported payment methods and other requirements may also vary.

No guaranteed earnings.

HennyMoney Afric does not guarantee a specific income, number of surveys, reward amount, payment method or redemption result from survey participation.

✅ Honest & Responsible Participation

Participants should provide truthful and consistent information when completing research surveys.

  • Do not intentionally provide false information.
  • Do not create multiple accounts when prohibited by the applicable provider.
  • Do not use automated methods, scripts or bots to manipulate survey participation unless expressly permitted by the provider.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate reward systems or referral programmes.
  • Follow the applicable survey provider's participation rules.

Violating a provider's rules may result in disqualification, loss of rewards or account restrictions imposed by that provider.

🔒 Privacy & Personal Information

A third-party survey provider may request personal or demographic information as part of its research process.

Before submitting personal information, participants should review the privacy policy of the relevant provider and understand how their information may be collected, processed or shared.

HennyMoney Afric does not ask participants to provide passwords, cryptocurrency private keys, recovery phrases or other highly sensitive account credentials through survey participation.

If a survey or offer requests information that appears unnecessary, unsafe or suspicious, stop and verify the provider before continuing.

🛡️ Online Safety

Participants should remain alert when using any online service, including survey and reward platforms.

  • Check the website address before entering information.
  • Avoid sharing passwords or one-time security codes.
  • Never disclose cryptocurrency private keys or recovery phrases.
  • Be cautious of websites requesting unnecessary payments to unlock supposed rewards.
  • Do not download suspicious software requested by an unverified survey.
  • Report or stop using an opportunity that appears fraudulent or misleading.

💳 Payments & Redemptions

Where a third-party programme offers a redemption or payment option, the relevant provider is responsible for its own payment processing, eligibility checks and redemption rules.

HennyMoney Afric does not guarantee the availability of a particular payment method such as PayPal, mobile money, bank transfer, gift cards or other payment services.

Available options may differ by country, provider, participant eligibility and programme terms.

⚠️ No Guarantee of Availability

Survey opportunities can change or become unavailable without notice.

The number of available surveys may depend on current research demand and participant eligibility.

HennyMoney Afric does not guarantee continuous availability of surveys or reward opportunities.

🌐 No Implied Endorsement

The appearance of a third-party survey, research provider, reward programme or external service on HennyMoney Afric does not automatically mean that HennyMoney Afric owns, operates or controls that service.

References to third-party services are provided for informational, community or participation purposes and remain subject to the third party's own policies and terms.

🔄 Changes to Survey Opportunities
