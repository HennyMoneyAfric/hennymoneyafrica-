🎵 Who Is HennyMoney Afric?

HennyMoney Afric is an independent Afrobeat artist and digital creator focused on connecting Africa with the world through music, storytelling, creativity and digital innovation.

From Burkina Faso, the HennyMoney Afric platform highlights African music, culture, tourism, creativity, entrepreneurship and digital opportunities while promoting positive African stories to a global audience.

🚀 Mission

The mission of HennyMoney Afric is to inspire people through music, creativity and cultural awareness while showcasing the diversity, talent and potential of Africa.

  • 🎶 Promote African music worldwide
  • 🌍 Highlight African culture and heritage
  • ✈️ Promote African tourism and travel discovery
  • 💡 Encourage digital creativity and innovation
  • 📚 Share useful creator and digital resources
  • 🤝 Build international connections and collaborations

🎤 Music Journey

Music is at the heart of the HennyMoney Afric brand. Through Afrobeat rhythms, African influences and inspirational messages, the goal is to celebrate African identity and connect listeners across cultures.

Featured music includes:

  • 🔥 Africa Unite
  • 🔥 God Bless Africa
  • 🔥 Proud Burkinabè
