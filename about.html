About HennyMoney Afric

Afrobeat Artist • Digital Creator • African Culture Ambassador

HennyMoney Afric is an independent African creative brand focused on music, culture, digital creativity and connecting Africa with the world.

My Story

HennyMoney Afric is an independent Afrobeat artist and digital creator from Burkina Faso, promoting African music, culture and creativity.

Through music, digital storytelling and creative projects, HennyMoney Afric aims to showcase African talent and share African stories with a global audience.

Mission

Connecting Africa to the world through music, culture, creativity, tourism and digital innovation.

Vision

Build a globally recognized African creative brand that inspires people, supports independent creativity and promotes African culture worldwide.
