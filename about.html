About HennyMoney Afric

Afrobeat Artist • Digital Creator

My Story

HennyMoney Afric is an independent Afrobeat artist from Burkina Faso promoting African music, culture and creativity.

Through music, digital creativity and online projects, HennyMoney Afric aims to showcase African talent and connect Africa with audiences around the world.

Mission

Connecting Africa to the world through music, culture, creativity and digital innovation.

Vision

Build a globally recognized African creative brand that celebrates African identity, supports independent creators and creates opportunities through digital platforms.
