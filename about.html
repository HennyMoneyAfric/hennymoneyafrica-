About HennyMoney Afric

Afrobeat Artist • Digital Creator • African Culture Ambassador

Connecting Africa to the world through music, creativity, culture, tourism and digital innovation.

My Story

HennyMoney Afric is an independent Afrobeat artist and digital creator from Burkina Faso. Through music, storytelling and digital projects, the mission is to showcase African talent and connect Africa with a global audience.

The brand focuses on creativity, culture, tourism, music promotion, education and empowering African creators through technology and digital opportunities.

Mission

To connect Africa to the world through music, culture, tourism, creativity and innovation while inspiring positive change and global collaboration.

Vision

To build a globally recognized African creative brand that promotes African excellence, inspires creators and creates opportunities for future generations.
