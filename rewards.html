🌍 Welcome to HennyMoney Afric Rewards

HennyMoney Afric is exploring responsible ways to connect members of its community with digital opportunities, including surveys and reward programmes provided by participating third-party research partners.

These opportunities may allow eligible participants to complete research activities and receive rewards according to the applicable programme rules.

Important: HennyMoney Afric does not guarantee that every visitor will qualify for a survey, receive an offer, or receive a specific amount or type of reward.

📝 How It Works

  1. Visit an available survey or reward opportunity.
  2. Review the eligibility requirements and programme terms.
  3. Complete the required research activity honestly and according to the applicable rules.
  4. If eligible, receive points or another reward according to the third-party programme's terms.
  5. Follow the applicable redemption instructions when you reach the required eligibility or redemption threshold.

🎯 Possible Opportunity Types

📋 Surveys

Research surveys may ask participants about opinions, consumer experiences, products, services or other research topics.

⭐ Points

Some programmes may use points or another reward system. Conversion values and requirements depend on the applicable provider.

🎁 Rewards

Depending on the programme and participant eligibility, available rewards may include different redemption options determined by the third-party provider.

👤 Eligibility

Survey availability can vary based on factors such as location, age, demographic requirements, research demand, survey capacity and other criteria established by the applicable research provider.

Being able to access a survey does not necessarily mean that a participant will qualify for the complete survey or receive a reward.

💰 About Rewards

Reward amounts, points, payment methods, minimum redemption thresholds, processing times and availability are determined by the relevant third-party programme.

Depending on the programme, available redemption methods may vary and can include options such as digital rewards, gift cards, payment services or other supported methods.

No guaranteed income: Survey participation should not be considered a guaranteed source of income. Do not pay money to someone who claims that payment is required to unlock a survey reward unless the applicable provider clearly confirms such a requirement.

🔐 Participant Safety

Protect your personal information when using online survey and reward services.

  • Never share passwords or account recovery codes.
  • Never share cryptocurrency private keys or recovery phrases.
  • Do not provide payment-card information unless you are certain you are using the legitimate provider's secure service.
  • Check the website address before entering sensitive information.
  • Review the privacy policy and terms of the applicable third-party provider.
  • Stop using an opportunity if you believe it is fraudulent, misleading or unsafe.

🤝 Third-Party Providers

Some survey and reward opportunities accessible through HennyMoney Afric may be provided, operated or fulfilled by independent third-party research companies.

HennyMoney Afric is not responsible for the eligibility decisions, survey availability, reward calculations, payment processing, account decisions or policies of independent third-party providers.

Participants should review the applicable provider's own terms, privacy policy and reward conditions before taking part.
